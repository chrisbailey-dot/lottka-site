What it does

Everything a desk needs, from the first lead to the placement.

Built by a recruiter with twenty years on the desk, for consultants who would rather be on the phone than in a spreadsheet.

Find leads

Watches careers pages, job boards and industry news, then ranks the employers under hiring pressure, with the reasons shown.

Outreach in your own voice

Drafts emails that sound like you, from your own mailbox. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

A proper CRM

Groups, sites and contacts, with terms that carry down from head office. Clients and candidates kept apart, the way they should be.

Candidates and jobs

Shortlists, send-outs and placements, with matching openings nearby whenever someone new applies.

Job ads everywhere

Your own jobs page, Google for Jobs, job board feeds and a jobs list for your website, with applications landing straight on the shortlist.

Win it retained

Spots the vacancies worth pitching as retained, then coaches you through the call with a script built from the role.

Several desks, one login

Run every market as its own business.

Each desk has its own Google mailbox and calendar, its own clients and candidates, and its own privacy rules for the country it works in. Nothing crosses between them.
