CRM and applicant tracking

Your CRM and your ATS, on one record.

Clients and contacts on one side, candidates and CVs on the other, jobs and placements between them. A new candidate lands and Lottka tells you which open jobs suit them. A new job lands and it tells you who to send.

If you have used any ATS, you will find your way round this one in an afternoon.

Outreach and floats

It writes the way you write.

Lottka learns your style from your own sent mail. Then it drafts your client emails and candidate floats in that style, takes the facts from the CV, and attaches the formatted CV, blind or named.

Three clicks from a candidate's page to a float in a hiring manager's inbox, sent from your own address.

Questions agencies ask us

Is Lottka an ATS or a CRM?
Both. It is an applicant tracking system for your candidates and jobs and a recruitment CRM for your clients and contacts, on one record.
What is the difference between an ATS and a recruitment CRM?
An ATS tracks candidates through jobs. A recruitment CRM tracks clients and the work you win from them. Agencies need both, and many products are called one or the other while doing much the same job. Lottka does both in one system.
Who is Lottka for?
Recruitment agencies placing permanent, temp or contract staff, in any sector.
How is it different from other recruitment agency software?
Most systems track work you have already found. Lottka also finds it, by watching your market and telling you who is hiring and who to call.
Can we bring our data from our current system?
Yes. Clients, contacts, candidates, CVs, jobs, notes and placements come across, and we help you do it.
Does it handle temp and contract recruitment?
Yes. Shifts, timesheets and pay files are part of the same system.
How is Lottka priced, and how long is the contract?
From £49 a seat a month, month by month or on twelve months. Seats go up and down with your team.
